在odoo18里，必须选中一个文件，才会显示出下载按钮，只需要修改以下代码即可

/mnt/odoo/odoo18/custom/addons/documents/static/src/views/search/documents_control_panel.xml 第114行附近

```python
                    = 1 or this.targetRecords.some((r) => r.data.attachment_id)"
                        t-on-click="() => this.onDownload()">
                        Download
                    button>
```
