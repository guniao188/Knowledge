# OCA Partner增加关联公司页签partner_affiliate

> [!info] 来源
> 开源智造论坛 · 模块库 | 作者:肖相扶 | 2022-12-15
> <http://www.thinkltd.cn/forum/2/oca-partnerpartner-affiliate-2710>

模块链接：

The field 'Contacts & Addresses' is focused on storing contact information about the company's employees and the company it self. This module adds a new field 'Affiliates' on the partner form and changes 'Contacts & Addresses' to only show individuals. This new field is in contrast to 'Contacts & Addresses' focussed on storing information about holding companies and other company structures.
