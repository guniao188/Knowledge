# OCA生产单MO上增加生产申请人字段mrp_stock_orderpoint_manual_procurement

> [!info] 来源
> 开源智造论坛 · 模块库 | 作者:肖相扶 | 2022-12-15
> <http://www.thinkltd.cn/forum/2/ocamomrp-stock-orderpoint-manual-procurement-3020>

模块链接：

本模块依赖模块：[基于订货规则手工订货stock_orderpoint_manual_procurement](http://www.thinkltd.cn/forum/2/question/ocastock-orderpoint-manual-procurement-2858)

当在订货规则上手工订货时候，如果是制造类商品，产生的MO上显示订货申请人。

This module updates a new field *Requested by* in Manufacturing Orders in the case the MO has been created as a result of a manual procurement from an orderpoint.
