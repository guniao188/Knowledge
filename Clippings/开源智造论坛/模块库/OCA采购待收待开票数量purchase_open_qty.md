# OCA采购待收待开票数量purchase_open_qty

> [!info] 来源
> 开源智造论坛 · 模块库 | 作者:肖相扶 | 2022-12-15
> <http://www.thinkltd.cn/forum/2/ocapurchase-open-qty-2624>

模块链接：https://github.com/OCA/purchase-workflow/tree/11.0/purchase_open_qty

PO列表视图增加待收货订单、待开票订单两个筛选项。PO Line上增加待收数量、待开票数量两个字段。

This module allows purchase users to identify what are the purchase orders that currently have quantities pending to invoice or to receive.
