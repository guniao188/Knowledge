# OCA列表视图Shift键多选web_listview_range_select

> [!info] 来源
> 开源智造论坛 · 模块库 | 作者:肖相扶 | 2022-12-15
> <http://www.thinkltd.cn/forum/2/ocashiftweb-listview-range-select-2832>

模块链接：

Enables selecting a range of records using the shift key.

1.  click a record;
2.  hold shift and click another record;
3.  you can repeat this operation as many times as you want.
