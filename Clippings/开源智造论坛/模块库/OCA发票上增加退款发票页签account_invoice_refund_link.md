# OCA发票上增加退款发票页签account_invoice_refund_link

> [!info] 来源
> 开源智造论坛 · 模块库 | 作者:肖相扶 | 2022-12-15
> <http://www.thinkltd.cn/forum/2/ocaaccount-invoice-refund-link-2705>

模块链接：

This module shows the links between refunds and their original invoices in the invoice form and also keep track of refund lines and their original invoice lines.

### Usage

This module creates in the invoice form a new 'Refunds' page when the invoice has some refunds. It shows a line for each refund invoice with main information.

For refunds, it shows in "Other information" page the refund reason and original invoice link.
