# OCA会计分录列表画面增加余额列account_balance_line

> [!info] 来源
> 开源智造论坛 · 模块库 | 作者:肖相扶 | 2022-12-15
> <http://www.thinkltd.cn/forum/2/ocaaccount-balance-line-2728>

模块链接：

This module adds a balance total for lines in move line view.

###

### Usage

1.  If you're an accounting adviser, enable developer mode and go to Accounting > Adviser > Journal items.
2.  Or open an account or a partner, and click on Action > Journal items.
3.  You will see a column named "Balance" with the balance of the line.
