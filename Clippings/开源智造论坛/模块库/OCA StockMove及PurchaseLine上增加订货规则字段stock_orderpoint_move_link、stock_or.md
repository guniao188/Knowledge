# OCA StockMove及PurchaseLine上增加订货规则字段stock_orderpoint_move_link、stock_orderpoint_purchase_link

> [!info] 来源
> 开源智造论坛 · 模块库 | 作者:肖相扶 | 2022-12-15
> <http://www.thinkltd.cn/forum/2/oca-stockmovepurchaselinestock-orderpoint-move-linkstock-orderpoint-purchase-link-2863>

模块链接：

This module adds to stock moves a direct link to the reordering rules that created them. In chained moves, the reordering rule is propagated.

 This module adds to purchase order lines a direct link to the reordering rules that created them. In chained moves, the reordering rule is propagated from stock moves to the purchase order line.
