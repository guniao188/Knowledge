【模块安装】

Odoo 13 20191002日的版本测试，该模块在社区版中安装时候报CSS错误，财务报表可以显示，但样式没有了。修正方法，参考链接：[/forum/1/question/odoo13error-undefined-variable-o-chatter-min-width-464](http://www.thinkltd.cn/forum/1/question/odoo13error-undefined-variable-o-chatter-min-width-464)

该模块安装了下面一些会计报表：

Accounting/Configuration/Management/Financial Reports
Accounting/Reporting/Audit Reports
Accounting/Reporting/Audit Reports/Consolidated Journals
Accounting/Reporting/Audit Reports/General Ledger
Accounting/Reporting/Audit Reports/Journals Audit
Accounting/Reporting/Audit Reports/Tax Report
Accounting/Reporting/Audit Reports/Trial Balance
Accounting/Reporting/Generic Statements/Balance Sheet
Accounting/Reporting/Generic Statements/Cash Flow Statement
Accounting/Reporting/Generic Statements/Executive Summary
Accounting/Reporting/Generic Statements/Profit and Loss
Accounting/Reporting/Management/Analytic Report
Accounting/Reporting/Partner Reports
Accounting/Reporting/Partner Reports/Aged Payable
Accounting/Reporting/Partner Reports/Aged Receivable
Accounting/Reporting/Partner Reports/Partner Ledger
