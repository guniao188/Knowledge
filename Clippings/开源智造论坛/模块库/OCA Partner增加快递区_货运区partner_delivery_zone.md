# OCA Partner增加快递区/货运区partner_delivery_zone

> [!info] 来源
> 开源智造论坛 · 模块库 | 作者:肖相扶 | 2022-12-15
> <http://www.thinkltd.cn/forum/2/oca-partner-partner-delivery-zone-2712>

模块链接：

This module allows to set delivery zones on partner. This information is written in sale orders and stock pickings. It also adds searches and groups in partners, sales orders and stock pickings views.

## [Usage](https://github.com/OCA/delivery-carrier/tree/11.0/partner_delivery_zone#id1)

To use this module you need to:

1.  Got to *Sales > Settings > Delivery Zones*
2.  Create some delivery zones.
3.  Go to *Sales > Customers*.
4.  Go to *Sales and Purchases* tab.
5.  Set a delivery zone for this partner in *Delivery Zone* field.
6.  Got to *Sales > Quotations*
7.  Create a quotation, automatically delivery zone field is filled. Confirm it.
8.  Open the picking and you can see the delivery zone in 'Aditional Information' tab.
